# ORVO24 — Director's Brief (v8)

**Target:** 20 sec · Apple / Airbnb / Linear tone · NOT AI commercial acting  
**Budget reality:** Real actors or pro editor required for full vision. Do NOT burn Replicate on full regen.

---

## Global rules

- Camera accidentally caught a real office moment — **not** performers selling to camera.
- Characters look at each other, **never** at lens.
- Smiles are **small**. Surprise = raised brows + tiny smile, not comic mouth-open.
- Office: 80% natural (white, wood, beige, grey, plants). Orvo24 orange only on laptop deck, small UI, end card.
- **Forbidden:** futuristic office, RGB, neon, blue screens, robots, AI influencer look, oily skin, exaggerated expressions.

---

## Cast

### Hero (woman, 27–30)
- Dark brown medium-length slightly wavy hair, real skin texture, light freckles, subtle crow's feet when smiling, natural brows, minimal makeup.
- Cream/white quality tee, wide beige trousers, small watch, white sneakers, simple canvas/leather bag.
- Must read as **real employee**, not model.

### Guy ("not five yet", 28–32)
- Short brown hair, very short uneven stubble, real pores, light forehead lines.
- Dark blue shirt, black pants, simple watch.
- Friendly, slightly cynical, nice.

### Background (each distinct, doing different things, never look at camera)
| # | Description |
|---|-------------|
| 3 | Woman 25–28, black hair in bun, round glasses, light green shirt |
| 4 | Man ~30, dark blond, grey polo |
| 5 | Woman 30–35, short hair, beige sweater, drinking coffee |
| 6 | Man 25–28, curly hair, white shirt, headphones around neck |

**Note:** Brief says 6 people at table; establishing mentions 6 laptops. (Earlier versions used 8 — **use 6 per this brief.**)

## What AI cannot deliver (honest)

- 6 consistent unique faces across cuts
- Natural micro-expressions and eye contact between characters
- Specific props/layout locked across shots
- "Apple commercial" acting

**Paths that can hit this brief:**
1. **Shoot with 6 actors** (~$500–3000) — best match
2. **Director + stock + heavy edit** (~$200–800) — partial match
3. **Replicate/Kling** — will look like v6 again; **do not recommend**
