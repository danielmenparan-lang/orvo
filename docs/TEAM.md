## Overnight protocol

1. Research wave → write reports
2. Ops synthesizer ranks top 10 changes
3. Implementation waves commit to `cursor/orvo-local-site-3bd5`
4. Judge re-reviews after each wave

## Honest limits

- Parallel specialist agents + scheduled follow-up waves — not a literal 20-person company office
- Marketing = assets & playbooks ready to publish (no posting to accounts without credentials)
- Quality bar = sharp niche MVP, not “big company overnight”
