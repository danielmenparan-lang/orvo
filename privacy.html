Privacy Policy

Draft stub · Global marketplace · Last updated 2026-08-23

ORVO processes account data (name, email), project briefs, messages, and payment metadata to operate the marketplace.

What we store

Profiles, requests, quotes, chat messages, and payment records in our database (Supabase). Payment card data is handled by Stripe when Checkout is enabled — not stored on ORVO servers.

Sharing

We share necessary project details with the other party on a deal (client ↔ assigned/quoted builder). We do not sell personal data.

Your rights

You may request access or deletion of your account data subject to legal and fraud-prevention limits.

Contact

Privacy requests: contact the ORVO operator via your account email channel.
