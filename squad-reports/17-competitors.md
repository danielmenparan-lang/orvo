## Battlecard: Upwork

### Who they are
Largest freelance marketplace. Clients post jobs; freelancers buy Connects and bid. Escrow / hourly / fixed. AI work is one category among thousands.

### Strengths
- Liquidity and brand trust at scale  
- Dispute tooling, contracts, Instant Pay  
- Buyers already have accounts  

### Weaknesses vs ORVO
- Noise: AI/WhatsApp experts buried under generalists  
- Connects + variable fees punish specialized IL builders  
- No Hebrew-first SMB journey; weak WhatsApp-API vertical  
- Clients pay extra marketplace fees (Basic ~3–5%, Business Plus ~8–10%) + initiation fees  

### When ORVO wins
- Buyer wants **WhatsApp / Hebrew automation specialists only**  
- Faster match without Connects arms race  
- Israel SMB who will not navigate Upwork English UI  
- Builders who want inbound niche demand, not proposal spam  

### When ORVO loses
- Buyer needs commodity design/dev at lowest global bid  
- Enterprise procurement already locked to Upwork  
- Buyer requires deep category history / long hourly retainers  

### Talk track
> “Upwork is a mall. ORVO is the aisle for AI agents and WhatsApp automation — vetted builders, no Connects tax.”

## Battlecard: ServedByAI

### Who they are
Marketplace **exclusively for AI services** (automation, chatbots/WhatsApp, content, ML, marketing, consulting). Verified providers, proposal pricing, Stripe escrow. **15% provider commission** (10% founding); **0% buyer fees**.

### Strengths
- Same category thesis as ORVO (AI-only marketplace)  
- Escrow + dispute narrative already built  
- Clear service taxonomy including Chatbots & Voice / WhatsApp  

### Weaknesses vs ORVO
- Global English product — not Israel / Hebrew / ILS beachhead  
- Provider-centric browse model vs ORVO’s **request → quote** flow (ORVO matches client intent first)  
- Broader “any AI service” vs ORVO’s sharper agent/automation hire loop  
- No local Meta BSP / IL channel partnerships  

### When ORVO wins
- IL / Hebrew WhatsApp jobs priced in ILS  
- Clients who prefer posting a brief and receiving quotes  
- Tighter product: agent delivery + demo links (ORVO chat rules already bias to GitHub/Vercel/n8n demos)  

### When ORVO loses
- Buyer wants broad AI consulting / content / marketing mix in one vendor search  
- Provider already earning on ServedByAI’s global demand  
- Buyer needs multi-currency enterprise vendor list today  

### Talk track
> “ServedByAI is the global AI bench. ORVO is the deal room for shipping an agent — especially WhatsApp in Israel.”

### Watch
- Their commission and escrow UX; copy what works, differentiate on **locale + request-first UX + agent demo link policy**.

## Battlecard: Gambot (גמבוט)

### Who they are
Israeli **Meta Business Solution Provider**. Hebrew UI, local support, WhatsApp API, visual bot builder, AI agent, CRM, campaigns, invoices, telephony. Plans from **₪179/mo**; 1-month free trial. Claims #1 WhatsApp API in Israel.

### Strengths
- Native Hebrew + IL trust + official API  
- Full stack: campaigns, Flows, multi-number, tax invoices  
- SMB can self-serve without hiring anyone  

### Weaknesses vs ORVO
- **Not a marketplace** — buyer still needs talent for custom AI, edge cases, CRM glue  
- Subscription + message volume; complex TCO  
- Does not solve “which freelancer do I trust with my number?”  

### When ORVO wins
- Custom Hebrew AI beyond templates; n8n/Make deep integrations  
- Buyer burned by DIY bot; wants fixed-scope build with escrow  
- Agencies who implement *on* Gambot / Cloud API and need leads  

### When ORVO loses
- Buyer only needs broadcasts + simple menu bot on a BSP  
- Procurement wants one vendor invoice for API + inbox forever  
- “No code in 5 minutes” promise is enough  

### Talk track
> “Gambot is the highway. ORVO finds the driver who builds your custom AI on it.”

### Partnership play
Tag builders “Gambot / Meta Cloud API certified”; co-marketing overflow custom work.

## Battlecard: Manychat

### Who they are
Flow-based automation for Instagram, Messenger, WhatsApp. Mar 2026 plans: Free / Essential / Pro / Business / Advanced. **WhatsApp requires Pro+** (~$29/mo annual-equivalent entry); AI often **+$29/mo** add-on. Meta template fees billed via wallet.

### Strengths
- Huge creator / social funnel ecosystem  
- Fast IG comment-to-DM plays  
- Mature flow builder and templates  

### Weaknesses vs ORVO
- English-first; Hebrew slang / IL SMB support weak  
- Flow + light AI ≠ custom agent with CRM/payments ownership  
- Pricing stack (plan + AI + Meta + overages) surprises SMBs  
- No vetted-builder marketplace  

### When ORVO wins
- Buyer needs **custom Hebrew agent**, calendar/CRM/payments, official API architecture advice  
- Project quote (₪k) beats learning Manychat + Meta compliance alone  
- Agency/builder delivery with escrow  

### When ORVO loses
- Creator wants IG growth loops and already lives in Manychat  
- Simple WA keyword flows under ~$50/mo tool budget  
- Non-IL English social-first brands  

### Talk track
> “Manychat automates DMs. ORVO ships the specialist who builds the agent your Israeli customers actually talk to.”

## Battlecard: Moltify

### Who they are
**AI agent marketplace** (beta): hire specialist agents per task (research, code, contracts, content). Pay per task ($2–$100+). Builders connect via webhook; keep **88%** (founding **94%** for 6 months). Escrow; unique **agent-to-agent hiring**.

### Strengths
- Clean “hire an agent, not a human” story  
- Attractive builder economics vs Fiverr/Upwork  
- Autonomous agent orchestration vision  

### Weaknesses vs ORVO
- Autonomous agents ≠ **WhatsApp Business API implementation for SMBs** (compliance, templates, Hebrew UX, human handoff)  
- Weak fit for relationship-heavy IL clinic/real-estate bots that need human builders  
- USD / global; no IL rails  
- Beta liquidity risk  

### When ORVO wins
- Jobs that need a **human builder** + WhatsApp BSP setup + Hebrew conversation design  
- Escrow around multi-day delivery with chat, revisions, demo links  
- Israel beachhead and ILS pricing  

### When ORVO loses
- Pure digital task (summarize PDF, one-shot research) with no channel deployment  
- Buyer wants fully autonomous agent swarm at $2–$20  
- Builder who only ships webhook agents, not client-facing WA  

### Talk track
> “Moltify hires agents for tasks. ORVO hires builders (and their agents) to go live on WhatsApp for real businesses.”

## When ORVO wins (summary)

1. **Scoped WhatsApp / AI agent builds** with Hebrew UX and official API  
2. Buyer wants **quotes from vetted specialists**, not Connects or DIY flows  
3. **Trust / escrow** between SMB and freelancer/agency  
4. Israel-first currency, RTL, and local channel GTM  
5. Demo-link-friendly pre-pay chat (product already encodes this)

## When ORVO loses (summary)

1. Buyer only needs **SaaS BSP subscription** (Gambot / Manychat / WATI)  
2. Commodity freelance labor at global lowest bid (Upwork)  
3. Pure **autonomous micro-tasks** (Moltify)  
4. Broad multi-category AI services catalog (ServedByAI) without IL angle  
5. No payment rail / fee clarity — until Stripe + ILS ship, trust narrative is incomplete
