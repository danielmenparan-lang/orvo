# 08 — GTM Lead: 30-Day Go-to-Market for ORVO

**Product:** ORVO — global marketplace to hire **vetted builders** for **custom AI agents** (post → quote → chat → pay).  
**Geography lock:** **GLOBAL** (English product UI). Regional channels (IL HE, LATAM ES, etc.) are optional acquisition — not product limits.  
**Live:** https://fantastic-eclair-0b2c66.netlify.app/  
**Window:** Day 0–30 (start: 2026-08-23).  
**North star (30d):** 40 waitlist/signups · 8 approved builders · 12 client requests · 3 paid or escrow-ready deals.  
**Liquidity rule (Role 06):** Seed builders first; do not advertise “browse jobs” until ≥8 approved builders can quote in ≤48h.

---

## 1. ICP (who we chase first)

### Primary client ICP — “Operator who needs a custom agent”
| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| Who | Founder / ops owner of SMB or small team — restaurant, clinic, salon, local service, SaaS ops — **anywhere** |
| Pain | Missed chats, manual booking/orders, FAQ spam, no night coverage, shady freelancer quotes |
| Budget signal | Already paying tools / freelancers; custom agents typically hundreds–thousands USD |
| Trigger | “I need an AI agent for WhatsApp / ops / CRM / voice” — not “I need AGI” |
| Language | **English-first** public GTM; local languages OK in delivery |
| Disqualify | Enterprise RFP hunters, pure research/hobby prompts, “build me ChatGPT clone,” DIY-only shoppers |

### Secondary client ICP — “Solo founder / small tech team”
Needs a custom agent (support, lead qual, ops) but won’t hire full-time. Global. Higher AOV later.

### Builder ICP — “Agent shippers, not resume polishers”
| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| Who | Freelancers / indie hackers who have shipped WhatsApp (Meta Cloud API / WABA), n8n/Make, LangChain/OpenAI, voice, CRM agents |
| Proof | Portfolio link, Loom of a live agent, or GitHub + client name |
| Motivation | Steady inbound jobs without cold DMs; hold→release narrative; founding cohort badge |
| Disqualify | “AI enthusiast” with no shipped work; agencies that only white-label ChatGPT; Upwork generalists |

**Wedge line (use everywhere — EN primary):**  
*“Need a custom AI agent? Post on ORVO. Vetted builders quote. Hire and pay safe — worldwide.”*  

Optional regional HE: *“צריכים סוכן AI מותאם? מפרסמים ב־ORVO. בונים מאומתים שולחים הצעות. משלמים בבטחה.”*

**Positioning lock:** ORVO sells the *builder* for custom AI agents — not another bot SaaS, not Upwork-for-everything.

## 2. Offers (what we sell in month 1)

| Offer | Audience | Promise | Ask | Price / mechanic |
|-------|----------|---------|-----|------------------|
| **A. Founding waitlist (clients)** | Operators worldwide | Early access + first request prioritized to top builders | Email + 1-line need | Free; optional “I can pay this month” checkbox |
| **B. Launch request credit** | First 20 clients who post | Founder personally matches 2–3 builders within 48h | Full request on product | Free matching (no discount wars) |
| **C. Founding builder cohort** | 10–15 builders | Badge + first dibs on matching jobs | Apply + portfolio | Free; manual vet; 48h SLA; **USD quotes** |
| **D. Concierge pilot** | 3 design-partner clients | Done-with-you scoping → posted ORVO request → hired builder | Intro call / DM | $0 platform fee on first deal (founder absorbs) to prove loop |
| **E. Builder referral** | Approved builders | Credit / intro for another approved builder who completes a job | Share link | Pay after first completed job |
| **F. Partner overflow (soft)** | BSP / agency partners | ORVO takes custom work they won’t staff | Intro handshake | Referral goodwill |

**Budget chips (EN / USD):** $500 · $1,500 · $3,500 · “custom”.  
Optional regional HE chips when posting to IL channels: ₪3,500 · ₪6,500 · ₪12,000.

**Do not offer in days 0–30:** marketplace discounts, open “anyone can bid,” agency white-label programs, broad “AI consulting,” or competing as a BSP.

## 3. Channels (global-first; regional optional)

| Priority | Channel | Role | Cadence |
|----------|---------|------|---------|
| P0 | **Product waitlist / signup on live site** | Capture demand | Always on |

| P2 | **Instagram / Facebook** | SMB owners; language per region | 2–3/week when capacity |
| P2 | **Agencies / accountants / BSPs** | Referral for “someone who builds the agent” | 5 intros/week |

**Channel rule:** Every post ends with one CTA — waitlist, Post a request, or Apply as builder. Never both primary CTAs in one asset.  
**Language rule:** Default public copy = **English**. Use HE/ES/etc. only when the channel is regional.

## 4. 30-day plan

### Week 1 — Supply + English wedge
- Approve 8–15 builders; founding cohort badge  
- EN LinkedIn/X daily; DM 30 builders + 15 clients globally  
- Optional: soft-share HE restaurant page only if doing IL outreach day  

### Week 2 — First requests
- Concierge match first posted requests  
- Publish `/for-builders.html` + homepage CTAs  
- Keep Stripe honesty (no fake escrow claims)

### Week 3 — Loop proof
- Push 3 deals toward funded/delivered  
- Double down on channels that produced signups  

### Week 4 — Tighten
- Kill weak channels; keep EN SEO + LinkedIn + founder DMs  
- Prep paid test only if creative + conversion proof exists
