# 08 — GTM Lead: 30-Day Go-to-Market for ORVO

**Product:** ORVO — vetted AI-agent marketplace (post → quote → chat → pay).  
**Beachhead niche (locked with Role 07):** Israeli SMBs hiring custom **WhatsApp AI agents** (restaurants, clinics, salons, local services) + Hebrew-capable builders who ship them.  
**Live:** https://fantastic-eclair-0b2c66.netlify.app/  
**Window:** Day 0–30 (start: 2026-08-23).  
**North star (30d):** 40 waitlist/signups · 8 approved builders · 12 client requests · 3 paid or escrow-ready deals.  
**Liquidity rule (Role 06):** Seed builders first; do not advertise “browse jobs” until ≥8 approved WhatsApp/SMB builders can quote in ≤48h.

---

## 1. ICP (who we chase first)

### Primary client ICP — “WhatsApp-first SMB operator”
| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| Who | Owner / ops manager of restaurant, café, clinic, salon, delivery, real-estate desk, or local service in Israel |
| Pain | Missed WhatsApp messages, manual order/booking taking, FAQ spam, no night coverage, shady freelancer quotes over WA |
| Budget signal | Already paying POS / CRM / freelancer; market anchors ~₪3,500–₪12,000 for custom bots (core AOV ~₪6,500–₪12,000) |
| Trigger | “I need a bot for WhatsApp / orders / appointments” — not “I need AGI” |
| Language | Hebrew primary; English OK for tech-forward founders |
| Disqualify | Enterprise RFP hunters, pure research/hobby prompts, “build me ChatGPT clone,” DIY SaaS shoppers who only want ₪179/mo templates |

### Secondary client ICP — “Solo founder / small tech team”
Needs a custom agent (support, lead qual, ops) but won’t hire full-time. EN+HE. Lower volume; higher AOV later. **Do not lead month-1 messaging with this.**

### Builder ICP — “Agent shippers, not resume polishers”
| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| Who | Freelancers / indie hackers who have shipped WhatsApp (Meta Cloud API / WABA), n8n/Make, LangChain/OpenAI, or Israeli BSP integrations (Gambot stack OK) |
| Proof | Portfolio link, Loom of a live agent, or GitHub + client name; can explain Hebrew edge cases + templates |
| Motivation | Steady inbound Hebrew jobs without cold DMs; escrow narrative; founding cohort badge |
| Disqualify | “AI enthusiast” with no shipped work; agencies that only white-label ChatGPT; Upwork generalists |

**Wedge line (use everywhere):**  
*“Need a WhatsApp AI agent for your Israeli business? Post on ORVO. Vetted builders quote. Pay safe.”*  
Hebrew: *“צריכים סוכן AI לוואטסאפ לעסק בישראל? מפרסמים ב־ORVO. בונים מאומתים שולחים הצעות. משלמים בבטחה.”*

**Positioning lock:** ORVO sells the *builder* for custom WhatsApp agents — not another BSP, not Upwork-for-everything. vs Gambot/Manychat = DIY SaaS; vs Upwork/ServedByAI = too broad.

## 2. Offers (what we sell in month 1)

| Offer | Audience | Promise | Ask | Price / mechanic |
|-------|----------|---------|-----|------------------|
| **A. Founding waitlist (clients)** | SMB owners | Early access + first request prioritized to top builders | Email + 1-line need | Free; optional “I can pay this month” checkbox |

**Do not offer in days 0–30:** marketplace discounts, open “anyone can bid,” agency white-label programs, broad “AI consulting,” or “#1 WhatsApp API בישראל” claims (that’s BSP territory).

**Goal:** 1–2 deals in progress · public social proof · referral ask.

| Focus | Actions |

### Week 4 — Amplify or cut (Days 22–30)
**Goal:** 40 total signups · decide paid channel · lock niche language.

| Focus | Actions |

**Exit criteria:** Written niche lock + channel kill list.

## 5. Waitlist mechanics

**Single form fields (keep short):**
1. Role: Client / Builder  
2. Email  
3. WhatsApp (optional, Israel preferred — `+972` / `05x`)  
4. One line: “What agent do you need?” or “What have you shipped?”  
5. Checkbox: “Ready in next 30 days”  
6. Optional: budget band (₪3.5k / ₪6.5k / ₪12k / not sure)

**Lifecycle:**
- Instant auto-reply (HE+EN): “You’re on the ORVO founding list. We match manually this month.”
- Day 2: Clients get “Post your request” deep link; builders get Apply link  
- Weekly digest: 3 open job types + CTA (copy in LAUNCH-KIT)  
- Promote waitlist members to full accounts as soon as they can self-serve

**Owner:** Founder reviews list daily; no CRM theater — Sheets/Supabase is enough.

## 6. Builder recruiting playbook

1. **Source list (50 names):** IL indie hackers, Upwork top WhatsApp freelancers (filter Hebrew), n8n/Make consultants, BSP freelancers, campus AI clubs with shipped demos.  
2. **Invite script (HE):** short — what ORVO is, vetting, no cold outreach, apply link, 48h review, ILS-friendly.  
3. **Vetting bar (all must pass):** shipped WA (or close) agent proof; can explain Meta Cloud API + templates + Hebrew; quotes with clear scope; accepts on-platform chat/pay; 24h first-quote SLA.  
4. **Onboarding:** skills tags, rate band (ILS), languages (HE/EN), availability hours, preferred verticals.  
5. **Activation:** send 1 matching open request within 24h of approval.  
6. **Retention:** weekly “new jobs” ping; founding badge; referral offer E; founding fee relief if product fee turns on.

**Cap month 1:** ~15 approved builders. Quality > fill. Reject out-of-niche “RAG for anything” until liquidity clears (~20 paid in-niche txns).
