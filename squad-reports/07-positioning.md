# 07 — Positioning

**Verdict:** Niche = **Israel WhatsApp AI agents for SMBs**. ORVO is the *hiring marketplace* for custom WhatsApp agents — not another bot SaaS, not Upwork-for-everything.

---

## Winner niche

### Pick

**ORVO = the marketplace where Israeli SMBs hire vetted builders to ship custom WhatsApp AI agents.**

Hebrew-first. Custom builds (n8n / API / LLM), not DIY drag-and-drop. Quote → chat → pay on ORVO.

### Why this wins over “general AI agent marketplace”

| Option | Verdict |
|--------|---------|
| **Israel WhatsApp / SMB agents** | **Winner.** Landing already demo’d it; clear buyer (restaurant/clinic owner); clear builder skill; local competitors are SaaS not marketplaces; price band known (₪3.5k–12k). |
| Global “any AI agent” | Loses to ServedByAI + Upwork on brand and supply. Cold start impossible. |
| US SMB WhatsApp agents | WA less central than IL; English crowded by Manychat agencies. |
| Internal enterprise agents | Long sales cycle; wrong for MVP escrow marketplace. |

**Sharpness test:** If you remove the logo, the first screen still says “WhatsApp agents for Israeli businesses” — not “AI marketplace.” Pass that or the niche is soft.

### Vs each competitor (one line)

- **vs Upwork:** Same “hire a human,” but ORVO is *only* WhatsApp agents for SMBs — no sorting 200 “AI expert” proposals.  
- **vs ServedByAI:** Same “vetted AI talent,” but ORVO is *geographically and channel-narrow* (IL + WhatsApp), not six AI service categories.  
- **vs Gambot:** Gambot sells the *platform*; ORVO sells the *builder* when DIY/SaaS isn’t enough (custom CRM, kitchen alerts, weird Hebrew flows).  
- **vs Manychat:** Manychat is self-serve English-first automation; ORVO is done-for-you custom agents with local builders and escrow.

### Positioning statement (locked)

> For Israeli SMBs who need a WhatsApp AI agent that fits their real ops, ORVO is the marketplace that matches them with vetted builders and protects payment — unlike Upwork (too broad), ServedByAI (too general AI), or Gambot/Manychat (DIY SaaS, not custom hire).

### Coopetition note (use in sales, not homepage)

Clients who outgrow Manychat/Gambot templates are **inbound for ORVO**. Pitch: “Keep the channel; hire a builder for the hard parts.” Do not attack BSPs — **refer when pure SaaS fits**, take the custom build when it doesn’t.
