Platform fees

Global marketplace · Updated 2026-08-23

Founding period

0% platform fee on quotes while ORVO is in founding mode. Clients pay the quoted amount; builders receive 100% of the quote (before Stripe processing fees when Checkout is live).

Later

Target fee is 10–12% of the quote, disclosed clearly before you accept & pay. Fee changes will not apply retroactively to already-accepted quotes without notice.

Payments

When Stripe Checkout is enabled, funds are held on ORVO until you release after delivery. See Terms.
